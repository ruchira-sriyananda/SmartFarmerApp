# Privacy Policy for Smart Farmer

**Last Updated: August 30, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Smart Farmer ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us to provide a better experience for all our users.

*   **Personal Information:** Name, Email address, and Phone number (collected during registration for account management).
*   **Location Information:** Approximate location (used to show relevant market and barter listings near you).
*   **App Usage & Performance:** Crash logs and Device IDs (collected via Firebase to improve app stability and security).
*   **User Content:** Posts, comments, and images you share in the community and market sections.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:
*   Create and manage your user account.
*   Provide app functionality, including the Market, Community, and Barter features.
*   Respond to your comments and questions.
*   Monitor and analyze app performance and stability.

## 3. Data Encryption & Security

All data collected by the Smart Farmer app is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS). We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access.

## 4. Account and Data Deletion

We provide users with full control over their data:

*   **In-App Deletion:** You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by navigating to **Settings > Delete Account** within the app.
*   **Web Request:** If you cannot access the app, you may request account and data deletion by sending an email to **[your-email@gmail.com]** (Please replace with your actual email).
*   **Types of Data Deleted:** Upon request, we will permanently delete your profile information, posts, comments, and history from our active databases.
